## RMSL201-1301 structured light module
### Description
Rmsl201-1301 is a fully functional structural light 3D camera module. Built-in speckle cast up to 30, 000 points
Emitter, 5 megapixel RGB camera, 1 megapixel global exposure infrared camera and infrared light source. Widely suitable
For face payment, access security, gesture, body recognition, high precision 3D modeling and other products.

### Product parameters
* Brand：Rockchip
* Model：RMSL201-1301
* Interface：USB
* Pixel：500W(RGB) 100W(IR)
* Supported WeChat face payment
