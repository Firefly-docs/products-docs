
# Power adapter

### Product parameter

* Product: Power adapter
* Specifications: US/EU standard
* Input standard: AC100-240V 50/60Hz
* Output standard: 12V-3A

<font color=#ff0000>Note: Face-RK3399 requires a 12V/3A power supply for normal operation. If the current is lower than 2A, it may restart abnormally. To ensure normal operation, use a 12V power supply with 2A to 3A current. The official Firefly power adapter is recommended.</font>
