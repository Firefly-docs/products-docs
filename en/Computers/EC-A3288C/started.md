
## Product introduction
EC-A3288C uses RK3288 quad-core Cortex-A17 processor, clocked at up to 1.8GHz, integrated quad-core Mali-T764 GPU, maximum support 4K hard solution, can achieve 4Kx2K H.264 and H.265 video hard decoding, supports the dual-screen identical/differential display, and the rich expansion interface meets the different practical needs of customers. At the same time, the beautiful aluminum alloy casing makes the product more perfect and simple, and the integrated overall design greatly shortens the customer development time period. Basically, it is a low-threshold high-performance product development tool.
