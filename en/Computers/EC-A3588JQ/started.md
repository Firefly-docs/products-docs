# Introduction

[Specification](https://download.t-firefly.com/Spec/Computers/EC-A3588Q_EC-A3588JQ_EC-A3588MQ_Specification_EN.pdf) | [Purchase](https://www.t-firefly.com/products/ec-a3588jq-octa-core-8k-ai-industrial-computer) | [Downloads](https://community.t-firefly.com/en/doc/download/203)

The EC-A3588JQ is an Octa-Core 8K AI Industrial Computer powered by the RK3588J octa-core 64-bit processor. It supports up to 32GB of RAM and 8K video encoding and decoding.
It features a wide range of interface options, including Gigabit Ethernet, WiFi6, 5G/4G expansion, multiple video input and output, as well as M.2 SATA/PCIe SSD. Multiple operating systems are also supported. With industrial-grade chips and precision components, it delivers long and stable operation at a temperature between -40°C and 85°C, satisfying industrial-grade needs.

## Interface Description

**** provides rich interfaces, mainly including:

* 1 x HDMI2.1 (up to 8K@60Hz output)
* 1 x HDMI2.0
* 1 x Display Port1.4 (up to 8K@30Hz output)
* 1 x USB3.0 OTG (Type-C)
* 1 x VGA
* 2 x MIPI-DSI
* 1 x MIPI-CSI
* 1 x EDP
* 1 x M.2 (SATA3.0/PCIe2.0)
* 1 x PCIe3.0x4
* 1 x TF Card
* 1 x 4G LTE
* 1 x 5G mobile network
* 1 x SIM card slot
* 4 x USB3.0
* 3 x USB2.0
* 1 x WIFI (supports WiFi6)
* 1 x Bluetooth
* 1 x FAN
* 2 x RJ45 (supports 1Gbps)
* 1 x Line-In
* 1 x RS485
* 2 x RS232
* 1 x CAN
