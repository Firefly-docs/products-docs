# Introduction

**CAM-1126BQ38** is powered by the Rockchip RV1126B quad-core AI vision processor with a 3 TOPS NPU. It supports 2B multimodal large models, 12MP video encoding, 4K video decoding, and includes a 12MP ISP / 8MP AI-ISP. It is suitable for facial recognition, access control, smart security, and intelligent network camera applications.

The **CAM-1126BQ38** kit is based on the ICORE-1126BQ38 system-on-module.
